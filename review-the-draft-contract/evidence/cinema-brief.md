# Silver Screen Cinemas: visitor service brief

QATBPAISP-M — Turn Business Problems into AI-Ready Specs

All company details, visitor messages, observations and examples in this workspace are fictional teaching evidence. They are not current cinema listings, real customer records or measured business results.

## The business problem

Silver Screen Cinemas is a small cinema company. Visitor service staff answer similar questions about access, screenings and bookings. The service manager reports that answers can vary between colleagues and that staff spend time checking several information sources. The company wants more consistent, useful replies without promising something it cannot deliver.

The original sponsor request is: “Could AI answer visitor questions and sort out their booking problems?” Treat this as a request to investigate, not an agreed solution or authorisation for automated customer service.

## Evidence from stakeholder conversations

| Stakeholder | Supplied observation or constraint |
| --- | --- |
| Visitor service manager | Repeated information questions are a useful starting point. Booking changes and refunds still need staff decisions. |
| Venue manager | Access information must be specific to the venue and screen. Two approved records disagree about Riverside Screen 3. The conflict has not been resolved. |
| Product manager | Start with a small pilot for staff-assisted drafting. A completed implementation and financial business case are outside this exercise. |
| Service supervisor | Staff must inspect every proposed reply before it is sent. A draft must show the facts used and the reason for a handoff. |
| Information owner | Only the supplied approved visitor information is in scope. It is a static snapshot. It cannot prove current seat availability, booking status or a visitor's eligibility for a refund. |
| Privacy lead | The proposed drafting input must contain no payment-card details, booking references or other unnecessary personal details. Upstream handling and staff actions remain human responsibilities in this exercise. |

These are supplied scenario statements, not verified research. There are no volumes, baseline timings, benefit measurements or budget approvals. Describe potential value in plain language; do not invent revenue gains or savings.

## A proportionate first slice

Investigate a staff-facing assistant that can draft answers to supported visitor information questions using approved facts. The staff member keeps responsibility for checking and sending. You may propose a narrower slice if you justify how it meets the business need.

The assistant does not send messages, make or change bookings, access payment records, issue refunds, infer accessibility needs or invent missing facts. There is no live booking system, schedule feed, external search, model account or software implementation in this lab.

## What you must decide

Separate the business problem from the sponsor's suggested solution. Break the work into tasks and decide which suit ordinary rules, an AI component or human judgement. Specify the input, output, constraints and success criteria for the AI-assisted drafting slice. Walk the specification through realistic visitor requests and record the changes needed before an engineering team could build a prototype.

The work is a specification review using supplied fictional evidence. A paper walkthrough cannot establish how a real model, service or business would perform.
