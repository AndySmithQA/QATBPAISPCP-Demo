# Supplied draft fixtures for review

These fictional model-style outputs were written for the exercise. No model was run. They illustrate possible design failures and do not establish the reliability or unreliability of any product. Review them against your specification; do not send them to visitors.

## Unsupported access assurance

Visitor request: “Can I reach Riverside Screen 3 without using stairs?”

Supplied output: “Yes. All Riverside screens have step-free access, so Screen 3 will be fine for you.”

Claimed source: Old generic access advert.

## Unauthorised booking promise

Visitor request: “Please move my Riverside booking to the later showing.”

Supplied output: “Your booking has been moved and your original seats will be kept.”

Claimed source: Booking change route.

## Unauthorised refund promise

Visitor request: “Please refund my ticket.”

Supplied output: “Your refund is confirmed.”

Claimed source: Refund decision route.

## A useful but incomplete draft

Visitor request: “Is there a step-free route to Screen 1 at Riverside?”

Supplied output: “There is a step-free route from the foyer to Screen 1 at Riverside.”

Claimed source: Riverside Screen 1 access.

The fixture has no status, reviewer handoff or source list outside the prose. Decide whether accurate wording alone is enough for the proposed staff-facing workflow.
